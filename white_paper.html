Bright Paper

We believe in tools, and the people who use them.
We believe in tools that foster autonomy, encourage inquisitiveness, strengthen personal agency, increase efficiency while acknowledging human pace and drive critical inquiry.
We believe knowledge is the essential human project.

We’re building beam because we think something is fundamentally broken in the way the tools at our disposal have us use the web and thus, our minds.
It feels like we have not freely chosen our online practices - that they are habits we have helplessly picked up.
We are not distributing our attention as we want.

Our tools define our relationship with the world.
Web browsers have framed our cognitive behavior to such an extent that we don’t question it anymore.
Can thinking be reduced to a search box with no memory or context? What is left of the thousands hours we spent on the web?

Sequentially typing searches in a vacuum is not how we think as humans.
We make the world our own by investing it with meaning, day after day.
We need to reconnect with the pleasure we derive from autonomously making sense of things and the ability to think, grow and build upon what we learned.

beam is a new breed of software. Not a note app, not a browser.
Searching and thinking should happen in the same medium at the same time. Not as an afterthought.

beam is a tool to navigate with intent, nurture your personal information landscape, become more discerning and demanding, hone your thinking and connect the dots.

We’re building beam as a tool for conviviality in the sense of Ivan Illich. We follow 4 principles:

  • Forthright: under your control and easy to master.
  • Proportionate: improve efficiency within the bounds of human aptitudes and pace.
  • Autonomy: what you do is what you get. No black magic.
  • Convivial: contribute to the knowledge loop: learn-create-share to the benefit of all
beam is a few months away.
We sincerely hope it will help you unleash the full power of your inquisitiveness and experience the web in a new way. The bright web.
